## Rev 2 — Ocarina input hotfix

Fixes Single Screen ocarina play ending early during longer songs such as
Bolero of Fire. Removes the automatic cancel triggered after six notes and a
short pause, so eight-note songs, held notes, and free play are not cut off.

Updated Mod and ROM Patch downloads for all five regions. The USA Mod fix
was confirmed in gameplay; all regional builds and ROM-patch round trips
were verified locally. Other regions have not had new gameplay testing for
this hotfix. The Korean Rev 1 fix is preserved. Dual Screen and experimental
Luma downloads are unchanged.

Mod users: replace your regional mod folder. ROM Patch users: apply the new
patch to your original clean ROM, not an already-patched ROM. Keep your saves.
The legacy USA ROM-patch download link also delivers this fix.
